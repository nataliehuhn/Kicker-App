Current standings

Rating is each player's live, permanent score, the same number shown on the Players tab. It updates the moment a match is entered anywhere in the app (from 1 to 10). A win against stronger opponents counts for more than one against weaker opponents, and close games move the rating much less than clear ones (a 10:9 counts for about a third of a 10:3). For 2v2 the team average is used; in a 2v1 the pair starts with a built-in head start, so the single player isn't marked down for an expected loss. The rating stays put even if the match is later cleared or reset. Played, wins and goal difference below only reflect matches still on record; use the toggle to include or exclude tournament matches (Teams and Rotation modes) from that count. Knockout matches involving the 3-player rotating team don't affect ratings, since it isn't clear who actually played.
